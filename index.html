Matched pathways

Every successful referral — before clearing

  • Complete a Paramedic Initiated Referral / Advice form and leave it with the patient.
  • Record P5 Referral to Care and document the RP number, handover details and outcome in eMR; obtain patient / person-responsible signature.
  • Advise to call back Triple Zero (000) if symptoms increase or the condition deteriorates before assessment.
  • If the referral is declined, attempt another suitable local pathway or a statewide virtual service; if transport is required, transport to the nearest appropriate hospital.
